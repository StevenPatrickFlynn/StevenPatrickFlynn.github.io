The formulas behind the picture

The group law above is Thangavelu's group law composed with $(z,t)\mapsto(\bar z,t)$. The vertical scale slider stretches the drawing only. Every formula and readout uses the true $t$.

Group and horizontal frame

On $\mathbb H^1=\mathbb C\times\mathbb R$, with $z=x+iy$,

\[(z,t)\cdot(w,s)=\big(z+w,\;t+s+\tfrac12\operatorname{Im}(\bar z w)\big),\] \[X=\partial_x-\tfrac12 y\,\partial_t,\quad Y=\partial_y+\tfrac12 x\,\partial_t,\quad [X,Y]=\partial_t .\]

$X$ and $Y$ are declared orthonormal. A curve is horizontal when $\dot t=\tfrac12(x\dot y-y\dot x)$, so $t$ grows by the signed area that $z$ sweeps out.

The center sliders apply left translation by $p=(a+ib,c)$:

\[(z,t)\mapsto\big(a+ib+z,\;c+t+\tfrac12(ay-bx)\big).\]

Geodesics from the origin

Unit speed, initial direction $e^{i\theta}$, charge $\lambda\in\mathbb R$:

\[z(s)=e^{i\theta}\,\frac{e^{i\lambda s}-1}{i\lambda},\qquad t(s)=\frac{\lambda s-\sin\lambda s}{2\lambda^{2}} .\]

For $\lambda=0$ this is the line $z=se^{i\theta}$, $t=0$. Otherwise $z$ runs round a circle of radius $1/|\lambda|$ through $0$.

The geodesic minimizes length exactly for $0\le s\le 2\pi/|\lambda|$. At $s=2\pi/|\lambda|$ it meets the $t$-axis at height $\operatorname{sgn}(\lambda)\,\pi/\lambda^{2}$. So the cut locus of $0$ is the $t$-axis minus $0$. In the view, the grey parts of the fan lie past this point.

Spheres

The sphere $S_r$ of radius $r$ is swept out by the endpoints of the minimizing geodesics of length $r$. With $\mu=\lambda r\in[-2\pi,2\pi]$,

\[|z|=r\,\frac{2\sin(\mu/2)}{\mu},\qquad t=r^{2}\,\frac{\mu-\sin\mu}{2\mu^{2}} .\]

So $S_r=\delta_r S_1$ with $\delta_r(z,t)=(rz,r^{2}t)$, and $S_r$ is a surface of revolution about the $t$-axis. The poles $(0,\pm r^{2}/4\pi)$ sit at the bottom of conical dimples, where the sphere is not smooth.

Helices and the X-ray transform

The geodesics of charge $\lambda\neq0$ through $p$ are the helices $s\mapsto p\cdot\gamma_{\theta,\lambda}(s)$, $s\in\mathbb R$. They have radius $1/|\lambda|$ and rise $\pi/\lambda^{2}$ per turn. The X-ray transform at charge $\lambda$ integrates over them:

\[I_{\theta,\lambda}f(p)=\int_{\mathbb R}f\big(p\cdot\gamma_{\theta,\lambda}(s)\big)\,ds .\]

Every helix of charge $\lambda$ through $p$ passes through the conjugate points $p\cdot(0,k\pi/\lambda^{2})$, $k\in\mathbb Z$. When $|\lambda|=2\pi/r$ the first of them are the poles of $p\cdot S_r$.

<p class="page__lead">I develop sharp estimates and microlocal tools for sub-elliptic operators: operators, such as sub-Laplacians on sub-Riemannian manifolds, which are not elliptic but are hypoelliptic under geometric conditions. For these operators the phase space carries a noncommutative group structure, characteristic varieties are singular, and dispersive behaviour differs fundamentally from the elliptic case. My work combines microlocal and semiclassical analysis with noncommutative harmonic analysis and representation theory.</p>

<section class="research-area" id="strichartz">
  <div class="ra-head">
    <span class="ra-number">01</span>
    <h3>Strichartz Estimates and Fourier Restriction for Sub-Laplacians</h3>
  </div>
  <p class="ra-item"><span class="ra-label ra-label--paper">Preprint</span> With <a href="https://www.math.unipd.it/~barilari/">Davide Barilari</a>, refined Strichartz estimates for the Schr&ouml;dinger and wave equations on Heisenberg and H-type groups, with no symmetry assumptions on the initial data (<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.04415">arXiv:2501.04415</a>). The main tool is a Fourier restriction theorem for the Heisenberg fan, the singular characteristic variety of the sub-Laplacian.</p>
  <p class="ra-item"><span class="ra-label ra-label--wip">In progress</span> Further consequences of this restriction-theoretic viewpoint for dispersive estimates in the sub-Riemannian setting.</p>
</section>

<section class="research-area" id="filtered">
  <div class="ra-head">
    <span class="ra-number">02</span>
    <h3>Microlocal Analysis on Filtered Manifolds</h3>
  </div>
  <p class="ra-item"><span class="ra-label ra-label--paper">To appear</span> With <a href="https://perso.math.u-pem.fr/fermanian.clotilde/">Clotilde Fermanian-Kammerer</a> and <a href="https://www.veroniquefischer.com">V&eacute;ronique Fischer</a>, a pseudodifferential calculus on filtered manifolds, quantizing operator-valued symbols in local adapted frames (<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.17448">arXiv:2412.17448</a>, to appear in <em>Memoirs of the European Mathematical Society</em>). </section>

<section class="research-area" id="spectral">

  </div>
  <p class="ra-item"><span class="ra-label ra-label--wip">In progress</span> Pointwise asymptotics of the spectral function of Schr&ouml;dinger operators with smooth bounded potentials, around a question of Galkowski, Parnovski and Shterenberg on complete asymptotic expansions.</p>
